Artisans & Guild

The Art of True Bespoke Joinery

Nivaraa Living is a family-founded bespoke furniture atelier based in Pune, dedicated to elevating everyday living through peerless craftsmanship. Our team of master woodworkers, tufters, and finishing artisans unite decades of experience. From kiln-dried Burma Teak and Sal wood foundations to couture Italian leather wrapping, every detail is calibrated to your home's exact architecture.

About Atelier

There’s no need to settle for anything less than perfection.

Nivaraa Living delivers a holistic and individualized home design experience, crafting bespoke sofas, modular sectionals, motorized recliners, and designer accent chairs that masterfully blend form with function.

Every piece reflects the distinct character and lifestyle needs of its owner. We consult with Pune's premier interior architects and discerning homeowners to ensure millimeter-accurate fit.
